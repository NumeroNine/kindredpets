In short

Kindred Pets stores your pets' log on your phone. The developer does not collect, send or share any data: the app has no account, no server, no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking services.

What data does the app handle, and where?

You enter the following data yourself. It stays exclusively in the app's own storage on your phone, which other apps cannot access:

  • your pets' details (name, species, breed, date of birth, sex, microchip number, coat colour);
  • your entries (feeding, vet visits, vaccinations, medication, weight, notes, habitat readings, etc.);
  • photos and files you attach (e.g. a pet's photo, the gallery, lab results, vaccination records);
  • your recurring tasks and reminder settings, and your absences (dates, type of care);
  • costs you record on entries;
  • your own contact details (phone number, email, emergency contact), if you enter them for the care sheet;
  • the app's settings (e.g. units, language).

The app does not transmit this data off the phone, except in the cases below that you start or switch on yourself.

When data does leave the app

  • Medical summary (PDF): when you ask for it, the app creates a PDF from your pet's data in the app's temporary folder (overwritten at the next export) and opens it in another app (a PDF viewer). Whether you then send or print it is your decision; that other app's rules apply.
  • Care sheet (PDF) and calendar export (.ics): when you ask for it, the app creates a PDF from your pet's data (and the contact details you entered), or a calendar file (.ics) from planned tasks and absences, and hands it over via the phone's share sheet to the app you choose (e.g. email, a messenger, a calendar). Where you send it is your decision; that other app's rules apply. The developer does not receive these.
  • Backup (only when you ask for it): in Settings the app saves all your data, documents and photos into a single file (zip). You take that file wherever you want via the phone's share sheet (e.g. Google Drive, email, a USB stick); the app does not upload it anywhere and the developer has no access. The file is not encrypted and may contain sensitive data (lab results, microchip number), so handle it carefully. The app does not make automatic cloud backups (Android's automatic backup is turned off). You can restore your data from a backup in Settings, for example on a new phone.
  • Payment (Pro): purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment data and the developer does not see it; about your Pro status we only know what Google Play reports about the purchase.

Permissions

  • Notifications and vibration: to show reminders (a notification may vibrate). Notifications are created on the phone, not sent from an external server.
  • Photos, camera, files: the app does not request general access to your photos or files. When you attach something, it uses the system's own pickers (photo picker, camera, file picker), and only the item you select is copied into the app's own storage.
  • At startup (after a reboot): to reschedule reminders.

Children

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your rights

Since the developer does not collect data, there is no data about you with us that we could provide or delete. You have full control over your data on the phone:

  • View, edit: in the app at any time.
  • Delete: by deleting an entry or a pet in the app, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the phone's settings (this deletes all data; backup files you saved are yours to manage).
  • Complaints: you can contact the address above, or the competent data protection supervisory authority (for the developer's location: Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA).

Changes

If this policy changes (for example due to a new feature such as cloud backup), we will publish the new version before the app update and update the date.
